## 1. Product definition

SoapCraft Pro is a free, anonymous-first system for an early-stage soap seller to formulate a recipe, size it, calculate true batch and saleable-unit cost, set wholesale pricing, plan an event, back-plan production, and purchase ingredients without entering the same facts repeatedly.

The product is not complete because an API or nominal page exists. Each public tool must complete a browser-observable loop: input, deterministic operation, result or useful error, math and assumptions, local restore/export/share, and a connected next action.

## 2. Goal system

**North star:** weekly anonymous utility sessions that produce a correct result and continue into another tool using inherited context.

**Activation:** a new visitor completes one full tool without authentication.

**Guardrails:**
- no chemical quantity generated by AI;
- no account, email, payment, or cookie gate for core results;
- no unsupported safety or financial recommendation;
- no silent missing cost, mixed currency, save failure, or last-write-wins conflict;
- no public chemistry result without release receipts;
- no completion claim without browser and deployment evidence.

## 3. Primary user and job

The primary user produces roughly 1–20 batches monthly and is planning recurring sales, a first market event, or a wholesale order.

> Given my recipe and selling plan, calculate every dependent quantity correctly, make the assumptions inspectable, preserve the context as I move to the next decision, and tell me when the answer is unavailable rather than inventing it.

Secondary users are careful beginners and experienced makers replacing spreadsheets. Cure readiness remains the user’s decision.

## 6. Recipe/Batch Context

The shared context is the seam between tools. It contains only fields entered by the user or explicitly accepted from a previous result, plus units, currency, calculator versions and source revisions. It may hold formulation lines, mold/calibration, saleable yield, cost basis, prices, event assumptions, production dates, stock and supplier offers.

Anonymous persistence is browser-local. Share/export is portable and strips personal information. A destination tool shows inherited fields and source, allows edits, and recalculates with its own current engine. Version mismatch produces a warning and explicit migration/recalculation choice.

## 7. Calculation and safety rules

`CALCULATION-SPEC.md` v2.0.0 is authoritative. In particular:
- positive explicit oil mass; no hidden 1000 g default;
- KOH-basis SAP manifest and versioned molecular-weight conversion for NaOH;
- superfat once before independent NaOH/KOH purity corrections;
- mixed-alkali KOH percentage is share of full pure equivalents;
- exactly one water mode;
- full precision internally, display rounding only;
- no arbitrary property ranges or oil-level IFRA logic;
- calibration before precise mold batter mass;
- separate markup and gross margin equations;
- ceiling rules for discrete units/packs/batches.

Safety, provenance or legal uncertainty fails closed. A disclaimer never substitutes for correct math.

## 8. States and failure behavior

Every tool supports `empty → editing → validating → result | warning | blocking_error`. Local actions support `unsaved → saving → saved | save_failed`; share supports `encoding → ready | too_large | decode_failed`. A failed operation preserves inputs and offers a recovery action.

The UI must distinguish complete from incomplete cost basis, precise calibrated output from planning range, estimated schedule from cure safety, and built-but-gated chemistry from a working public calculator.

## 9. Non-functional requirements

- keyboard complete; 44 px minimum touch controls; screen-reader labels; status not conveyed by color alone;
- responsive at narrow mobile, tablet and desktop viewports without horizontal overflow;
- deterministic engines isolated from React and API adapters;
- client/server validation parity;
- no recipe, cost or supplier data in analytics;
- print/export output includes units, assumptions, revision and generated date;
- canonical metadata and structured data describe only available behavior;
- production build, lint, typecheck, unit/integration/e2e, accessibility and secret scans pass.

## 10. Deferred scope

Optional account/cloud sync may reuse existing NextAuth and Neon/Drizzle after the anonymous tool chain works. Seller Pack may reuse Dodo behind an idempotent verified webhook after demand is observed. Subscription billing, CRM automation, social scheduling, community, marketplace, ecommerce integrations, AI chemistry, and native apps are deferred and cannot block the eight tools.

## 11. Release gates

Engineering status is independent from publication status. `CHEM-GATE` requires authoritative ingredient manifest provenance, conflict dispositions, hand calculations, independent calculator comparisons, exact fragrance certificate handling, and named domain/safety review. Other tools require deterministic fixtures and deployed browser evidence.

Gate_A remains `PENDING` until an independent verifier records the literal `RELEASE_ACCEPTED`.
